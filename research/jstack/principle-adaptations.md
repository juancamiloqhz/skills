## Shared differences from the pin

- Standalone installation is user-approved. Relative sibling links become named skills resolved through advertised catalog paths. A dependency paragraph tells callers to report missing or ambiguous helpers, preserve independent work and leave installation manual. Two named helpers remain unimplemented: benchmark-checklist and typescript-best-practices. Show-me-your-work is now a [standalone mapped package](../../skills/jstack/show-me-your-work/SKILL.md), with [restoration evidence](evaluations/trail-restoration/README.md). All three source directories remain watched as context for their consuming principles.
- Every leaf retains a short scope/authorization paragraph, preserves unrelated work and repository constraints, and makes clear that direct use does not activate juan-mode or change configuration. This implements the user's standalone-use and authority decisions without deleting upstream algorithms.
- Descriptions are restored from the pin. The existing omission of `disable-model-invocation: true` remains pending a provider invocation-policy investigation. This PR restores bodies; it does not claim omission is required by T3 or prove automatic discovery. The unresolved metadata difference is explicit, not a completed fidelity correction.
- Blank lines and a local Markdown lint directive preserve the original emphasis and list structure. No provenance section is added to operational skills.
