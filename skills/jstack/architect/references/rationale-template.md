# Rationale shape

Keep the rationale proportional to the design, usually about a page. Candidates leave the final synthesis decision to the coordinator.

- Problem and grounded constraints, with source references and unresolved evidence.
- Usage first, with representative caller examples including failure behavior.
- Shape, including types, signatures, ownership, data flow, invariants and hidden complexity. Use one sketch for a small change or a module map for larger work.
- Synthesis decision, including base candidate, comparison and judge findings, selected contributions and rejected changes.
- Tradeoffs and red-flag findings, including what the design deliberately exposes to callers.
- Structurally different alternatives and why their ownership or interface costs were rejected.
- Verification performed, remaining risks or open questions, and the next implementation step if implementation is in scope.
