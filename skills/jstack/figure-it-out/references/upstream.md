Preserves framing, risk-scaled rigor, workflow design, per-unit hypothesis and measurement, a decision trail and final product verification. Standalone packaging supports direct custom-workflow requests and catalog-discovered callers. Normal discovery allows callers; merely reading the source does not activate juan-mode.

The decision trail is a bundled reference, not a separately installed show-me-your-work dependency. Parent-owned writes and bounded current-thread evidence replace transcript-directory assumptions. Trail review uses the existing scalar judgment role; inherited same-model review is disclosed instead of silently forcing another family. Canonical setup and T3 lifecycle replace fixed provider assumptions.

Planning-only requests return before product edits. Existing authorization and explicit checkpoints control execution rather than redundant approval for reversible steps. Recovery preserves unrelated work; no automatic rebase, reset, publication or installation is required. The seven relevant principles are discovered as standalone skills with their own attribution; their bodies are not duplicated here. This does not make the 23 playbooks implemented.
